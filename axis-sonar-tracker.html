What is the Community Sale

Powered by Sonar

The Community Sale is how the public buys AXIS at a fixed price — $0.10 per token — by committing USDC on Base. It runs on Sonar, Echo's token-sale infrastructure, which handles sign-in, identity verification, and eligibility on Axis's behalf.

1

Register

Sign in on Sonar — new or returning, one flow.

2

Verify

Complete KYC (individuals) or KYB (businesses) on Sonar. Identity documents never touch Axis — Axis only reads the eligibility result you authorize.

3

Commit

Once verified, commit USDC on Base directly to the sale contract. Eligibility comes from your verified Sonar entity — connecting a wallet alone does not make you eligible.

AXIS is an ERC-20 on Base, hard-capped at 1,000,000,000 total supply. U.S. persons and 27 restricted jurisdictions cannot participate (offered under Regulation S). This is not investment advice — read the investor disclaimer before committing any funds.
